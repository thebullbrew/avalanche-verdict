The Method

Real simulation, not a rule of thumb. Each strategy is simulated month by month: interest accrues on every balance at APR ÷ 12, minimums are paid on everything, and the remaining budget attacks one target at a time. When a debt dies, its freed minimum joins the attack. Minimums-only never rolls anything — freed money is treated as spent, which is what actually happens to most people.

Avalanche targets the highest APR first — mathematically the cheapest order, always. Snowball targets the smallest balance first — it costs a little interest and buys quick wins. This tool prices that trade honestly: if the snowball's interest penalty is small and it kills a debt months sooner, the verdict says so. Motivation you actually use beats a perfect plan you quit in March.

One warning: never let the order debate delay the extra payment. The gap between minimums-only and either method dwarfs the gap between the two methods. Pick the winner above, automate it, and go look at houses.
